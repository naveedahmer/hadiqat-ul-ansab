# Hadiqat-ul-Ansab · حدیقۃ الانساب

*The Garden of Genealogies* — a handwritten Urdu genealogy of the Syed families of Muzaffarpur (Bihar), compiled by **Syed Jaleel Akhtar** son of Syed Jameel Akhtar over fifty years (1925–1975). This copy was written out by the author in 1975 at the request of his nephew, Syed Muhammad Yusuf son of Syed Dabir-ul-Haq.
